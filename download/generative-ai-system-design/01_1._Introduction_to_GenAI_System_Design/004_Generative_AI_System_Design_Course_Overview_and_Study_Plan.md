# Course Structure and Study Roadmap

Explore the comprehensive course layout for mastering Generative AI System Design. Understand the layered approach from foundational concepts to real-world design problems and interview preparation. Learn how to navigate the course effectively based on your experience and goals to optimize your preparation for GenAI system design interviews.

We'll cover the following...

- [Study roadmaps](https://www.educative.io/courses/generative-ai-system-design/course-structure-and-study-roadmap#Study-roadmaps)
- [Strengths of This Course](https://www.educative.io/courses/generative-ai-system-design/course-structure-and-study-roadmap#Strengths-of-This-Course)

Think of the course as a deployment pipeline for your interview reasoning. Each layer feeds the next, so later design problems reuse earlier decisions instead of forcing fresh thinking every time.

The course is organized into seven core layers.

- **Foundations:** The introduction and GenAI Foundations chapters supply the minimum model knowledge, from core ML concepts and key GenAI design concepts to post-training and reasoning models, needed to reason about generation without hand-waving.
- **Nonfunctional characteristics:** These lessons introduce quality, groundedness, latency, throughput, and cost as design constraints, and show where an LLM fits and where deterministic logic wins.
- **Estimation:** These lessons translate product scope into rough numbers for training compute, inference capacity, fleet size, and token-level unit economics.
- **Building blocks:** Four chapters introduce reusable modules: retrieval and context, model serving and optimization, evaluation, observability, and guardrails, and agentic components such as memory, tools, planning loops, and durable execution.
- **Design framework:** The SCALED framework provides a repeatable interview flow for unseen problems, built on four recurring patterns and mapped to the RESHADED approach.
- **Design problems:** These lessons combine all earlier layers into full systems across four chapters: core GenAI systems (text, image, speech, and video generation, image captioning, and speech recognition), modern GenAI systems (memory, document Q&A, code assistance, search, unified retrieval, and voice), agentic systems, and AI platforms and infrastructure.
- **Interview practice:** Five mock interviews (RAG and Finetuning, ChatGPT, DALL·E, ElevenLabs, and Sora), placed at the end of major sections, along with the final chapter, shift the focus from knowing components to communicating decisions under a clock.

## Study roadmaps[#](https://www.educative.io/courses/generative-ai-system-design/course-structure-and-study-roadmap#Study-roadmaps)

Following every chapter in order works best for most learners, but the course also supports shorter paths depending on experience and available time.

- **New to GenAI**: Following the chapters in order pays off, since each design problem reuses the foundations, estimation habits, and building blocks introduced earlier.
- **Experienced ML engineers**: Skimming GenAI Foundations leaves more time for the nonfunctional characteristics, estimation, and building block chapters before the SCALED framework and the design problems.
- **Short on time**: The SCALED Framework chapter, one or two problems from each design chapter, the mock interviews, and the Interview Practice and Conclusion chapter form the fastest path to interview readiness.
- **Refreshers**: The free GenAI System Design lessons at the end of the course are optional refreshers on topics such as hardware choices, the training loop, diffusion, and agent architectures.

With the structure and study paths in place, let's look at what sets this course apart.
