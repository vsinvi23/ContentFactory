## Staying ready for interviews[#](https://www.educative.io/courses/forward-deployed-engineer/whats-next#Staying-ready-for-interviews)

The time between finishing a course and walking into an interview matters. Three habits keep the preparation sharp.

- **Keep the company stacks current:** SDKs, models, and prices change quickly, so we check each company’s current documentation in the week before an interview.
- **Repeat the practice rounds:** We rerun mock interviews and design rounds under time, then score each attempt with the self-review rubric.
- **Rebuild one take-home:** Building one assignment from scratch within its time limit shows where our process still slows down.

## What’s next for the FDE[#](https://www.educative.io/courses/forward-deployed-engineer/whats-next#Whats-next-for-the-FDE)

The role is still evolving, and the choices made in the years ahead will shape what kind of FDE we become. Here are four directions worth thinking about.

- **Domain expertise:** The FDE who knows one vertical deeply, whether healthcare, legal, or financial services, arrives at every project in that domain already understanding the data governance landscape, the compliance requirements, and the workflow patterns that first-timers encounter in week two. Pick one vertical and go deep. Each project adds context the next one builds on.
- **Evaluation fluency:** As agentic systems become more capable and take more autonomous actions, the ability to design evaluation suites and validate system behavior before deployment becomes the skill that separates reliable deployments from unpredictable ones. FDEs who can define what “good” looks like for a specific system, measure it, and defend that measurement to a skeptical stakeholder are the ones organizations will bring back for their most important projects.
- **From building to orchestrating:** The FDE’s job will increasingly involve designing the workflow and validating the judgment of capable agentic systems rather than writing every component from scratch. The execution layer is getting automated. The judgment about what to build, how to scope it, and whether it is working as intended stays with the FDE.
- **A personal playbook:** After every project, we spend thirty minutes capturing what worked, what we would ask earlier next time, and what we would do differently. Over three or four projects, this becomes a compounding asset that no course can provide. The FDE who systematizes their own learning from the field builds an advantage that grows with every engagement.

Some of those directions call for greater technical skills, and the courses below are a good place to build them.

## Courses to go deeper[#](https://www.educative.io/courses/forward-deployed-engineer/whats-next#Courses-to-go-deeper)

Each area below matches a skill this course builds on. The linked courses go further than this course does.

- **LLMs and APIs:** Calling model APIs in code, handling streaming responses, managing context windows, and reasoning about token costs and latency.

  - [Building with OpenAI](https://www.educative.io/courses/building-with-openai): Covers working with the OpenAI API end to end, from basic completions to function calling and advanced API patterns.
- **Prompt engineering:** Writing effective system prompts and few-shot examples, getting structured output reliably, and adapting prompts based on observed failures.

  - [Prompt Engineering Guide](https://www.educative.io/courses/prompt-engineering-guide): Covers structured prompting techniques, chain-of-thought reasoning, and prompt evaluation methods.
- **RAG:** Building a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipeline end-to-end and diagnosing failure modes under real data conditions.

  - [RAG with LLMs](https://www.educative.io/courses/rag-llm): Covers building retrieval pipelines from scratch, including indexing, querying, and output grounding.
  - [Vector Database](https://www.educative.io/courses/vector-database): Covers vector storage, similarity search, and embedding-based retrieval in depth.
- **Agentic frameworks:** Building agents with tool use and multi-step orchestration, and debugging agent failures in code.

  - [LangChain LLM](https://www.educative.io/courses/langchain-llm): Covers building LLM-powered applications and agents using LangChain.
  - [LangGraph](https://www.educative.io/courses/langgraph-from-langchain-user-to-agent-builder): Covers stateful, graph-based agent architectures for production agentic systems.
  - [Google Agent Development Kit](https://www.educative.io/courses/google-agent-development-kit): Covers building and deploying AI agents using Google’s ADK.
  - [Agent-to-Agent Protocol](https://www.educative.io/courses/agent2agent-protocol): Covers multi-agent communication patterns and the A2A protocol.
  - [Advanced Model Context Protocol](https://www.educative.io/courses/advanced-model-context-protocol): Covers MCP for tool integration and context management in agent systems.
- **Building agentic systems:** Architecting, evaluating, and deploying multi-agent AI systems in production.

  - [Agentic AI Systems](https://www.educative.io/courses/agentic-ai-systems): Covers end-to-end design and deployment of production agentic systems.
  - [Agentic Design Patterns](https://www.educative.io/courses/agentic-design-patterns): Covers common architectural patterns for reliable and scalable agentic workflows.
- **LLM evaluation:** Measuring model output quality with a systematic eval suite and knowing where automated evals fall short.

  - [LLM Evaluation](https://www.educative.io/courses/llm-evaluation): Covers evaluation frameworks, benchmark design, and automated testing methods for LLM outputs.
- **LLMOps:** Deployment, latency management, cost tracking, and observability for AI systems in production.

  - [LLMOps](https://www.educative.io/courses/llmops): Covers the operational side of running LLM applications at scale, including monitoring, versioning, and cost management.

The habits that matter most compound quietly. Scoping tightly, communicating honestly, documenting clearly, and leaving behind systems that the customer team can genuinely own. The more deliberate the practice, the more durable the results.

Good luck in the field. The work an FDE does matters for the systems that ship, and even more for the organizations and people who depend on them.
